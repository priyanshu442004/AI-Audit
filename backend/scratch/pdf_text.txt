2. Detailed File Analysis & Audit Impact
1. Item Master ISPL.xls   |  Role: Material & Item Master
 ■ HIGH IMPACT
What this file is for: List of all raw materials, components, and items purchased or manufactured.
The Problem Found: Severe Missing Data: Over 70% of active items present in purchase receipts (GRPO) are
missing from this master list. Out of 3,773 item codes in GRPO, 2,701 items do not exist in this file.
What it Causes in Audit: The audit system cannot verify item descriptions, standard unit prices, or categories for
missing items. Quantity and rate variance reports will flag these items as 'Unregistered Master Items'.
Action Required: Request your IT/SAP team to download a complete, updated export of the Item Master containing
all currently active item codes.

2. GRPO Report Item Wise.xls   |  Role: Goods Receipt (GRN)
 ■ HIGH IMPACT
What this file is for: Record of all physical goods received and accepted at the factory warehouse.
The Problem Found: Missing Column: The 'Purchase Order Number' (PO No) column is missing from this file
export.
What it Causes in Audit: The system cannot directly link received goods back to their original Purchase Order
numbers. 3-Way Matching (PO vs Goods Receipt vs Invoice) cannot automatically verify if correct PO quantities were
received.
Action Required: Ask your IT team to include the 'PO No' (or Base PO Ref) column in the GRPO report export
query.
3. Purchase Register Item Wise ISPL-C.xls   |  Role: Purchase Invoices
 ■ HIGH IMPACT
What this file is for: Complete register of vendor tax invoices entered into accounting.
The Problem Found: Missing Column: The 'Purchase Order Number' (PO No) column is missing from this register.
What it Causes in Audit: Invoice line items cannot be automatically cross-matched to originating PO numbers,
making it difficult to detect over-billing against PO price limits.
Action Required: Ask your IT team to add the 'PO No' column into the Purchase Register export query.
4. BP MASTER-S.xls   |  Role: Vendor Master
 ■ MEDIUM IMPACT
What this file is for: Directory of all approved suppliers, vendors, and business partners.
The Problem Found: Missing MSME Field & Vendor Code: No MSME / Udyam registration classification column
(Micro, Small, Medium, Non-MSME) is provided. Additionally, active vendor V000128 is missing from this file.
What it Causes in Audit: The system cannot automatically classify vendors under the legal 45-day MSME payment
rule. Transactions for vendor V000128 will show as 'Unregistered Vendor'.
Action Required: Provide an updated Vendor Master file that includes the MSME Category column and includes
vendor V000128.
5. Gate Entry Report.xls   |  Role: Security Gate Entry Log
 ■ MEDIUM IMPACT
What this file is for: Record of physical trucks and material inward entries logged at factory security gates.
The Problem Found: Missing Bill Date & Unmatched POs: Lacks Vendor Bill Date column. Furthermore, 222 PO
numbers referenced at the gate (e.g. PO #13267, #13054) do not exist in the Purchase Order Report file.
What it Causes in Audit: Cannot calculate exact delivery delay between Vendor Invoice Date and Gate Entry Date.
Gate entries for those 222 POs cannot be verified against official POs.
Action Required: Ask IT to include 'Vendor Bill Date' in the gate entry export, and check if those 222 POs belong to
another branch or series.
6. General Ledger.xls   |  Role: Accounting Journal Ledger
 ■ MEDIUM IMPACT
What this file is for: Detailed financial transaction journal showing all account entries and payments.

The Problem Found: Missing Vendor Code Column: The file exports GL Offset Account numbers instead of
